Coconut Dr. Pepper Dirty Soda Recipe

Coconut Dr. Pepper Dirty Soda

A fun twist on the original dirty soda, this recipe calls for Dr. Pepper instead of Diet Coke.

Ingredients

  • 7.5 ounces Dr. Pepper
  • 2 tsp vanilla syrup
  • 2 tbsp coconut syrup
  • 1-2 tbsp half-and-half

Instructions

  1. Fill a glass with ice and pour in soda
  2. Stir in syrup
  3. Add cream
  4. Enjoy!
