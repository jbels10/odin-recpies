Raspberry Cream Soda Recipe

Raspberry Cream Dirty Soda

A refreshing berries and cream style mix. A little fruity plus a little sweet.

Ingredients

  • 7.5 ounces Sprite
  • 2 tsp vanilla syrup
  • 2 tbsp raspberry syrup
  • 1-2 tbsp half-and-half

Instructions

  1. Fill a glass with ice and pour in soda
  2. Stir in syrups
  3. Add cream
  4. Enjoy!
